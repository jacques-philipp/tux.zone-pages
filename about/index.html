About Jacques

Platform engineer, Linux nerd, and accidental adult.

Hello there, human (or bot) 👋#

I’m Jacques Philipp - a platform engineer, Linux whisperer, open source crusader, and part-time chaos consultant. I spend my days knee-deep in Kubernetes clusters and my nights wondering why YAML exists. Welcome to my lair - I mean, my personal site.

👨‍💻 What I Actually Do#

  • Senior Kubernetes Platform Engineer @ LSD Open
  • Builder of reliable (but occasionally stubborn) platforms using OpenShift, Linux, Ansible, and Bash
  • Resident problem-solver, documentation nerd, and “why-is-this-alert-firing” detective
  • Strong believer that everything can be automated — except good coffee

I work in the wild world of containers, clouds, and controlled anarchy. I break things for a living. Then I fix them. Sometimes in that order.

🧠 Credentials, Street-Cred, & Chaos Energy#

  • Red Hat Certified (working my way to RHCA with my team of rebels 🏴‍☠️)
  • Deep love for Fedora, KVM, Cockpit, and duct-taping Linux together in home labs
  • Currently obsessed with: Tailscale, WireGuard, OpenWRT, and remote Kubernetes cluster access with minimal swearing

You’ll find me talking about everything from OpenShift health reporting, alerting stacks, and Kubernetes VPN overlays, to weird Bash scripts that shouldn’t work (but do).

🧘 Work-Life-Insanity Balance#

When I’m not convincing Prometheus to calm down or battling YAML demons, I’m likely:

  • 🌿 Working on a laptop in a forest
  • 🧠 Writing witty AI prompts or rants about tech
  • 🐧 Naming my servers ridiculous things like overcompensator.core.whore
  • 👨‍❤️‍👨 Spending time with my husband Elmar (aka the real sysadmin at home)

I’m equal parts tech nerd, nature enthusiast, foodie and meme connoisseur.
